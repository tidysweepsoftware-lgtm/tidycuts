# Code of conduct

TidyCuts is for people who make videos and people who build the tools they use.
Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or experience.

- Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention, in any form.
- Keep it useful: stay on the topic of the project in issues and reviews.
- Respect other people's time. A short, clear report beats a long angry one.

If someone breaks these rules, the maintainers may edit or remove their
comments, or ask them to leave the project spaces. To report a problem, use the
private **Report a vulnerability** form on the Security tab (see SECURITY.md) and
say it's about conduct, or ask a maintainer to contact you.
